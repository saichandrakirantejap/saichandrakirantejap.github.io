// 01 · About

Who I Am

A data scientist who focuses on decisions, not just tools.

I started as an Associate Data Scientist at DBS Bank in Hyderabad, where I built XGBoost fraud detection models from scratch — handling class imbalance with SMOTE, engineering 200+ features in PySpark, and explaining model decisions to compliance teams using SHAP.

I then moved to the US to pursue an MS in Data Science at the University of Alabama at Birmingham, where my capstone involved building a Deep Convolutional GAN for anime face generation in PyTorch.

Most recently I worked at Docusign on a multi-agent document processing system — designing the full data layer from Kafka MSK ingestion and semantic chunking to hybrid RAG retrieval, LangGraph agent orchestration, and end-to-end observability via LangSmith and CloudWatch.

I care about why semantic chunking over fixed-size, why hybrid retrieval over dense-only, why tune on precision-recall rather than accuracy — more than just which tools were used.
